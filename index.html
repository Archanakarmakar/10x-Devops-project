Statue of Liberty

The Statue of Liberty is an iconic symbol of freedom located on Liberty Island in New York Harbor. It was a gift from France and is a popular stop for first-time visitors to New York City.

Best time to visit

Spring through fall (April–October) for milder weather; expect crowds in summer.

Quick tip

Book ferry tickets in advance and arrive early to avoid long lines.

Times Square

Times Square is a bustling commercial intersection in Midtown Manhattan known for bright lights, billboards, theatres, and the New Year's Eve ball drop.

Best time to visit

Evenings year-round for neon lights; expect heavy pedestrian traffic.

Quick tip

Keep belongings secure and step aside to take photos — sidewalks get busy.

Grand Canyon

The Grand Canyon in Arizona is a vast, dramatic gorge carved by the Colorado River. It's famous for layered bands of red rock revealing millions of years of geological history.

Best time to visit

Spring and fall for comfortable temperatures; summer can be very hot on the rims and inner canyon.

Quick tip

Bring water, wear sturdy shoes, and check trail difficulty before hiking.

Yellowstone National Park

Yellowstone is America's first national park, known for geysers (including Old Faithful), hot springs, wildlife, and scenic landscapes spanning Wyoming, Montana, and Idaho.

Best time to visit

Late spring through early fall; winter offers a very different (snowy) experience with guided tours.

Quick tip

Stay on boardwalks near thermal areas and keep safe distance from wildlife.

Walt Disney World

Walt Disney World Resort in Orlando is a large family-friendly entertainment complex with theme parks, water parks, hotels, and dining.

Best time to visit

Late fall and early spring for lower crowds and pleasant weather; holidays are busiest.

Quick tip

Plan ahead using park apps and reserve popular attractions early.

Golden Gate Bridge

The Golden Gate Bridge is an internationally recognized symbol of San Francisco, famous for its red-orange towers and sweeping bay views.

Best time to visit

Spring through fall; mornings can be foggy, afternoons often clear.

Quick tip

Walk or bike across the bridge for great photo opportunities; dress warmly for winds.

Las Vegas Strip

The Las Vegas Strip is known for large casino resorts, shows, nightlife, and themed attractions along a lively boulevard.

Best time to visit

Fall through spring to avoid extreme summer heat; weekends are busiest.

Quick tip

Compare show prices and book ahead for popular performances.

Niagara Falls

Niagara Falls is a set of powerful waterfalls on the US–Canada border, famous for boat tours, observation points, and dramatic scenery.

Best time to visit

Late spring to early fall for boat tours and warmer weather.

Quick tip

Bring a waterproof layer if you take a boat close to the falls.

Hollywood Walk of Fame

The Hollywood Walk of Fame celebrates film, television, music, and theater with stars embedded in the sidewalks along Hollywood Boulevard in Los Angeles.

Best time to visit

Year-round; mornings are quieter for photos.

Quick tip

Combine with visits to nearby theaters and the TCL Chinese Theatre for a fuller experience.

Mount Rushmore

Mount Rushmore in South Dakota features giant carved faces of four U.S. presidents and offers historical exhibits and scenic viewpoints.

Best time to visit

Late spring through early fall for warmer weather and open visitor amenities.

Quick tip

Check for ranger programs and the evening lighting ceremony when available.
